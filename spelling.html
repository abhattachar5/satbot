Year 6 SATS Spelling — FAQs

How does the KS2 SATS Spelling test work?

In the KS2 Spelling test, each word is read aloud in a sentence with a pause where the target word should go. Students write the missing word. SATBot replicates this exactly — click "Hear word" and write what you hear.

How many words are in the Year 6 Spelling SATS?

The real Year 6 Spelling SATS (SPaG Paper 2) contains 20 words and lasts 15 minutes. Each SATBot spelling practice set also contains 20 words presented in the same audio format.

Does the Spelling test count towards the SATS score?

Yes — the Spelling test (SPaG Paper 2) contributes to the overall SPaG score. Both Paper 1 (Grammar) and Paper 2 (Spelling) marks are combined for the final KS2 SPaG result.

How can I improve my spelling for Year 6 SATS?

The most effective preparation is practising with words read aloud in sentences, exactly as in the real exam. SATBot's audio-based Year 6 SATS spelling practice covers multiple year sets so you can work through different word lists regularly.
